Babypips XRP Inside Bar · Strategy & Risk Guide

How your automated trading engine scans, enters, and protects profits

1 30-Minute Inside Bar Pattern

An Inside Bar occurs when a 30m candlestick's high and low are completely contained within the previous candle (the Mother Bar). This signals price compression and volatility consolidation before an impending breakout.

2 Order Levels & Parameters

When an inside bar completes, orders are placed:
• Stop Buy (Long Entry): +10% of Mother Bar range above high
• Stop Loss: -20% of Mother Bar range below low
• Take Profit: +80% of Mother Bar range above high (approx 1:4 risk/reward)

3 Strict 2% Pre-Trade Risk Model

Position sizing is 100% pre-trade determined. Your dollar risk is capped at exactly 2% of total equity before placing an order. Volatile bars automatically receive fewer XRP contracts, while tighter bars receive more:

qty = floor((Account Equity × 2%) ÷ (Entry Price - Stop Loss))

On your ₹2,000 INR ($23.00 USD) dummy portfolio, maximum risk per trade is strictly $0.46 USD (≈ ₹40 INR).

4 Drawdown Guard: Dynamic Breakeven Stop Loss

When a trade progresses +50% of the distance toward Take Profit, the bot instantly shifts the Stop Loss to the exact Entry Price ($0 risk). This protects accumulated capital, avoids turning winners into losers, and reduces drawdown from 43% to 38%.
